ASTM A193 Grade B7 Technical Data

Grade B7 is a quenched-and-tempered chromium-molybdenum alloy steel (AISI 4140 / 42CrMo4) with a minimum tensile strength of 125 ksi (860 MPa), used for high-temperature and high-pressure bolting.

Heat treatment

Oil quenched and tempered at 593 °C (1100 °F) minimum, ensuring uniform hardness through section.

Thread standards

UNC / UNF (ASME B1.1), metric coarse and fine (ISO 261), BSW / BSF. Class 2A / 6g fits standard.

Finishes

Plain / black oxide, hot dip galvanised (ASTM A153), zinc plated, PTFE / Xylan 1070, cadmium plated.

Matching nuts

ASTM A194 Grade 2H heavy hex nuts, and Grade 7 / 4 where specified by the flange class.

Temperature range

Suitable for service from -29 °C up to 400 °C; use L7 / L7M for low-temperature duty.

Certification

EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificates with chemical, mechanical, hardness and dimensional results.
