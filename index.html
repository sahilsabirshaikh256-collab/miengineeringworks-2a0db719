About us

Precision Fasteners from Mumbai's Iron Market

M.I. Engineering Works is a Mumbai-based manufacturer, supplier and exporter of high-tensile industrial fasteners. From our own CNC machining, threading and heat-treatment facility we produce ASTM A193 Grade B7 stud bolts, hex bolts, threaded rods and anchor bolts for refineries, power plants, EPC contractors and fabricators.

Every batch ships with full traceability and EN 10204 3.1 material test certificates. Custom sizes, special threads and coatings are made to order with short lead times.

FAQ

ASTM A193 B7 Questions

What is ASTM A193 Grade B7?

A chromium-molybdenum alloy steel (AISI 4140/4142), quenched and tempered, with 860 MPa minimum tensile strength for high-pressure, high-temperature bolting.

What sizes do you manufacture?

M6 to M100 (1/4 inch to 4 inch) in lengths from 30 mm to 3000 mm, plus custom sizes and special threads on request.

Do you provide test certificates?

Yes — EN 10204 3.1 material test certificates with chemical, mechanical and hardness results for every batch.

Do you export fasteners?

Yes. We supply across India and export to the Gulf, Africa, Europe and South-East Asia with export documentation.
