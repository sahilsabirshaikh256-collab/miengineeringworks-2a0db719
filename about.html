About M.I. Engineering Works

A Mumbai fastener manufacturer trusted by refineries, power plants, EPC contractors and fabricators across India and overseas.

Who we are

Operating from the Iron Market in Girgaon, Mumbai, M.I. Engineering Works manufactures and supplies the full range of industrial fasteners — stud bolts, hex bolts, heavy hex bolts, socket products, anchor bolts, threaded rods and round bars — primarily in ASTM A193 Grade B7 chromium-molybdenum alloy steel.

Our facility covers CNC turning, thread rolling and cutting, hot and cold forging support, quenching and tempering, and finishing such as hot dip galvanising, zinc plating and PTFE/Xylan coating. Dimensional, hardness and mechanical checks are carried out batch by batch.

Quality policy

We commit to supplying fasteners that meet or exceed the declared standard for every order, with documented traceability from raw material heat number to dispatch. Non-conformities are contained, recorded and corrected, and customer feedback drives continual improvement of our processes.

Material traceability

Heat numbers recorded from raw bar to finished fastener, with EN 10204 3.1 certificates.

Testing

Tensile, yield, elongation, hardness and impact testing through accredited laboratories.

Third-party inspection

TPI by LRQA, BV, SGS, TUV and client inspectors welcomed on request.

Global supply

Domestic dispatch across India plus exports with complete shipping documentation.

Custom manufacturing

Non-standard lengths, special threads and drawings-based production.

On-time delivery

Planned production slots and stock of common B7 sizes for fast turnaround.
